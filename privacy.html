3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you via your Access Key and maintain your session
  • To display your content (posts, messages, signals) to other users within your cubes
  • To calculate and display your CUBE token balance and rewards
  • To provide real-time communication via WebSocket connections
  • To detect and prevent abuse, spam, and unauthorized access
  • To improve platform performance and fix bugs

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising profiling.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Render.com (backend infrastructure). Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+.

Your Access Key is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. If you lose your key, we cannot recover your account — there is no email-based reset. Please keep your key safe.

End-to-end encryption (E2E) is available for direct messages when the E2E mode is enabled by both parties.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store:

  • Your Access Key and authentication tokens
  • Your preferences (last viewed cube, display settings)
  • Cached messages and cube visit history

This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to third parties. You can clear it at any time via your browser settings, which will log you out of the platform.

7. Cryptocurrency & Wallet Data

CubeWorld includes a built-in CUBE token system. Please note:

  • Your CUBE token balance is stored on our servers linked to your Access Key
  • If you connect an external wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, etc.), we store only your public wallet address. We never have access to your private key or seed phrase.
  • Cryptocurrency transactions are irreversible. We are not responsible for losses due to user error.
  • CUBE token prices displayed are indicative only and do not constitute financial advice.

8. Children's Privacy

CubeWorld is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account promptly.

Users between 13 and 18 must have parental consent to use cryptocurrency features.

9. Your Rights (GDPR & Global)

If you are located in the European Union or other jurisdictions with data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — Request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Right to deletion — Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Right to rectification — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to portability — Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object — Object to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at snna.anna.she@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Since CubeWorld is anonymous by design, we identify your account by your Access Key. Please include it when making a rights request.

10. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Inactive accounts: Accounts inactive for more than 24 months may be deleted
  • Deleted accounts: All data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Server logs: Technical logs are retained for up to 90 days for security purposes

11. Changes to This Policy

For significant changes, we will notify users through an in-app notice.
